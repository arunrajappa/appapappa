Ik Onkar

Mool Mantar from the Japji Sahib Link to heading

There is One God!

He is the supreme truth

He, the Creator

Is without fear and without hate

He, the Omnipresent,

Pervades the universe.

He is not born,

Nor does he die to be born again.

By his grace,

shalt thou worship him

Before time itself

There was truth!

When time began to run its course

He was the truth!

And, Evermore shall truth prevail

–
