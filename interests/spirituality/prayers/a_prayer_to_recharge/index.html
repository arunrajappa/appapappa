A Prayer to Recharge

By Paramahansa Yogananda Link to heading

“Heavenly Father, it is Thou

Who dost directly sustain my body.

Awaken within me

Conscious will, conscious health,

Conscious vitality, conscious realization.

O Eternal Youth of body and mind!

Abide in me forever and forever”
