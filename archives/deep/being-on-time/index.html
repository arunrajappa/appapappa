Time is all we’ve got. Roughly 25,000 days, if we’re lucky, about a third of that spent in sleep.

So, when someone is late on us, they are literally killing us, taking away a part of our lives and squashing it underfoot with no care or concern. It is something each of us should get angry about, when someone wastes our time.

Most of all though, we waste it ourselves, till it’s too late.

We waste our time tolerating toxic people, tolerating unnecessary meeetings, tolerating parties we don’t want to be at - all to be nice (at the cost of dying a little).

The only way to live life is to enjoy and be fully present at each moment.
