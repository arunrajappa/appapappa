**Books on my TOREAD list for 2025**

1. 📘 Lonesome Dove – Larry McMurtry
Epic Western of love, loss, and legacy. Heart-forward and unforgettable.
(Reading before your birthday)

2. 📕 Life and Fate – Vasily Grossman
A war novel that wrestles with power, morality, and humanity on a massive scale.
(Reading before your birthday)

3. 📗 In Search of Lost Time – Marcel Proust
A layered meditation on memory, identity, and the passing of time.
(Reading from August to December)

4. 📜 The Odyssey – Homer
The foundational epic — about return, transformation, and endurance.

5. 🌅 East of Eden – John Steinbeck
A generational saga about good and evil, with one of the most moving themes on free will.

6. 🌀 2666 – Roberto Bolaño
A modern labyrinth of evil, literature, and violence in a postmodern noir package.

7. 🧠 Infinite Jest – David Foster Wallace
Addiction, irony, and meaning in the age of overthinking — a maximalist brainbreaker.

8. 🎭 The Copenhagen Trilogy – Tove Ditlevsen
Memoir-trilogy of girlhood, ambition, and addiction — searingly honest and poetic.

9. 🔥 The Hour of the Star or Agua Viva – Clarice Lispector
Interior, mystical, radical. For when you want feeling without plot, and fire without explanation.
